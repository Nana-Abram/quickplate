# Photo credits

The restaurants in QuickPlate's sample data are invented. The food photos are real and openly licensed. They come from Wikimedia Commons, Flickr and the WordPress Photo Directory, and the app loads them from those sites by URL. The photos are not edited; the app only crops them to fit its layout. The login screen's photo (`assets/images/hero_plate.jpg`, the same photo as `friedRiceSaladChicken`) is bundled with the app, resized.
